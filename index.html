Privacy Policy

PushyUncommit  ·  Last updated: April 26, 2026  ·  Developer: SudarshanTechLabs

Short version: PushyUncommit does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All communication stays between your phone and your own machine on your local network.

Overview

PushyUncommit is a developer tool that connects to a Python server you run on your own computer. The app shows you the status of your git repositories. No data is sent to any third-party server, and no personal information is collected by SudarshanTechLabs.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location
  • Your contacts or calendar
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Any content from your git repositories

Permissions

The app requests only:

  • Internet — required to connect to the PushyUncommit Python server running on your local machine or network. All traffic goes directly to a server you control; no data passes through any external server.

Local Storage

The app stores your server URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.x:8080) locally on your device using Android's AsyncStorage so you don't have to re-enter it every time. This data never leaves your device.

Your Own Server

PushyUncommit communicates with a Python server you install and run yourself (python -m PushyUncommit --web). You are in full control of that server and any data it processes. SudarshanTechLabs has no access to your server or its data.

Third-Party Services

The mobile app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or advertising frameworks. There are no embedded trackers of any kind.

Children's Privacy

PushyUncommit is a developer tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
