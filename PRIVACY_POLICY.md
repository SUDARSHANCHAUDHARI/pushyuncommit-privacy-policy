# PushyUncommit Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 2026
_Last updated: April 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

PushyUncommit ("we," "our," or "us") is a developer tool for Android that connects to a Python server you run on your own machine to show the status of your git repositories. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

**SudarshanTechLabs does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.**

- No account or registration required.
- No analytics or crash reporting.
- No advertising SDKs.
- No third-party SDKs or embedded trackers of any kind.

### Data Stored Locally
- Your server URL (e.g. `http://192.168.1.x:8080`) is stored locally on your device so you don't need to re-enter it each session.

## How We Use Your Information

The app communicates exclusively with a Python server **you install and run on your own machine**. You are in full control of that server and any data it processes. SudarshanTechLabs has no access to your server or its data.

## Storage and Retention

- Only your server URL is stored locally on your device.
- All local data is cleared when you uninstall the app.

## Data Security

- Communication between the app and your local server stays within your own network.
- No data passes through any external server.

## Permissions Used

- **INTERNET**: Required to connect to the PushyUncommit Python server running on your local machine or network.

No other permissions are requested.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Clear stored server URL via Android Settings → Apps → PushyUncommit → Clear Data.
- Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

## Children's Privacy

PushyUncommit is a developer tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
